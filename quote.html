Before your first order

Do you sell partial loads?

Full truckloads are our focus. Partial loads may be possible depending on the product, destination and freight options.

How do credit and terms work?

Our receivables are credit-insured. We request a credit limit for your account through our insurer, and once it’s approved we extend that line to you. Standard terms are 1% 10 days — a 1% discount on the lumber portion of the invoice (not freight) when paid within 10 days.

Where do you deliver?

Our core market is the Southeast, and we serve customers in other states including Texas and Kansas. Heavy green products are best suited to Southern destinations.

Who arranges trucking?

It depends on the order. Some mills ship directly; otherwise we coordinate through our trucking contacts. Freight is included in your delivered price.

Can you handle recurring orders?

Yes — many customers reorder the same material regularly. Availability and pricing are checked for each load.

What if there’s a problem with a load?

Call us. We’ll review the issue with you and the parties involved and stay with it until it’s resolved.
