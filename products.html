Our core business

Pallet, crate & industrial packaging lumber

Keep your production lines moving. We supply pallet and crate manufacturers, pallet recyclers and industrial packaging plants that buy lumber week after week — and we’re built for those repeat truckloads.

Quote pallet lumber

Truss manufacturers

Structural Southern Yellow Pine dimension lumber for truss and component plants. Send your sizes, lengths and grade — including MSR and No. 1/No. 2 — and we’ll quote it delivered.

Dunnage

Economical boards, timbers and landscape timbers for blocking, bracing and protecting loads in transit. Ask about green material for cost-conscious dunnage programs.

Playground manufacturers

High-grade lumber for play structures. We remanufacture it to your requirements and send it for pressure treatment.

  • 4x4, 2x4 and 1x4 — other sizes on request
  • Free of knots and wane
  • Smooth, eased edges
  • Pressure treated

Landscape timbers

Green, untreated timbers that come directly from plywood mills, bought by pallet grading companies, dunnage users and remanufacturing plants. We also remanufacture them into boards for pallet manufacturers.

Because green material is heavy, this product is offered to buyers in the Southern United States.
