How we work

Know the delivered price. Know what happens next.

A lumber order is more than a mill price. Availability, freight and timing all matter — we handle those details and keep you informed.

  1. 01

    Send your requirements

    Species, dimensions, lengths, grade, quantity, treatment and delivery ZIP — or paste in your material list.

  2. 02

    We check mills and freight

    We contact suitable mills in our network and price trucking to your location — whether the mill ships or we arrange it.

  3. 03

    Review one delivered price

    Often the same day. If a better-value option exists we’ll show it — any change only with your approval.

  4. 04

    Track it to arrival

    Updates on material readiness, trucking and expected arrival — and a person who stays involved if anything needs attention.

About Timber Resources

Sawmill experience. A family business that stays involved.

Ken Proctor has worked in the lumber industry since 1981, for sawmills across the country — including a year with a spruce manufacturer in British Columbia. He started Timber Resources in 2012 to put that mill-side knowledge to work for buyers.
